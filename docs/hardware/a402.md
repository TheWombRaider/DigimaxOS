## Factory test mode (documented, not explored)

The service manual describes a test mode: remove the card, hold OK while pressing POWER for about
2 s, wait for the beep, then press RIGHT. Its adjustment list includes firmware update, "PUNT",
"MSCALIB" (probably mechanical shutter calibration), battery and bad-pixel calibration.
**Do not enter it.** Spec section 7A forbids running calibration or firmware functions in order
to explore them. Even observing the menu needs the owner's explicit approval, and nothing in it
may be selected.
